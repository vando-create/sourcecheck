Check a smart form against its source PDFs

Drop the exported form JSON and the sub docs. SourceCheck lines up every question, option, paragraph and footnote with the PDF text and flags what differs, what is missing, and checkboxes that print into the wrong box. Runs in your browser; files are not uploaded.

Source PDFs

Every sub doc the form was built from. Each is matched to its annotated PDF by name.

Add a form JSON and at least one PDF.

The demo uses a made-up fund, “Northwind Credit Fund I”, with seven issues planted in the form: swapped Yes/No boxes, Regulation D changed to S, $200,000 changed to $300,000, an edited footnote, a missing AML paragraph, a helper message, and one acceptable wording change.

Text differs

Form wording found in the PDF but changed: dropped words, altered terms, wrong footnotes.

Missing in form

PDF text on a digitized page that no form element carries.

Form-only text

Text in the form that the PDFs do not contain: custom language or rewrites to verify against the checklist.

Swapped mapping

A checkbox option that prints next to another option's text in the PDF, such as Yes and No mapped the wrong way round.

AI triage settings

AI triage sends the selected findings (short text snippets and diffs, not the whole files) to Claude, which marks each one as a likely bug or an acceptable form adaptation. Your key stays in this browser. Check that your team allows sending client document text to an external AI service.
